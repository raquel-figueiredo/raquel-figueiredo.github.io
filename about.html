I'm a Senior Product Designer with 12+ years of experience across healthcare, insurance, IoT, gaming, enterprise software, and consumer products. I lead end-to-end design work — from discovery and systems thinking through prototyping, validation, delivery, and post-launch iteration.

My strongest work sits at the intersection of user needs, business rules, and technical constraints. I've designed for patients, employees, nurses, insurance analysts, auditors, customer-support teams, brokers, and connected-home customers — often within the same ecosystem.

My path here wasn't linear. I started in IT support and front-end development before moving into UX — time at TOTVS, Viceri Seidor, and a Computer Science degree that still shows up in how I think about data models and edge cases, not just screens. That technical grounding, combined with a later Executive MBA in Marketing and Innovation Strategies, is part of why I'm comfortable owning both the systems thinking and the business conversation around a product, not just its interface.

How I work

01

Frame

Clarify the business problem, users, constraints, risks, and decision owners.

02

Discover

Combine research, stakeholder knowledge, analytics when available, legacy-product analysis, and benchmarks.

03

Model

Map journeys, rules, states, roles, edge cases, and system dependencies before polishing UI.

04

Explore

Use wireframes, prototypes, and AI-assisted workflows to evaluate multiple directions quickly.

05

Align

Make decisions visible, explain UX rationale, and negotiate trade-offs with Product and Engineering.

06

Deliver

Create production-ready flows, responsive designs, components, specifications, and handoff documentation.

07

Learn

Use operational feedback, support patterns, experiments, and product outcomes to guide iteration.
